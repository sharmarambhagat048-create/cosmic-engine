Exotic Propulsion CONCEPT

Antimatter
Propulsion

The most energy-dense fuel physics allows: complete conversion of mass to energy.

How it works

Matter–antimatter annihilation converts 100% of rest mass into energy (E = mc²), about 9×10¹⁶ J/kg, roughly 10¹⁰ times chemical propellants. Proton–antiproton annihilation yields charged pions that can be steered magnetically.

The barrier is production and storage. Global antiproton output is on the order of nanograms per year at enormous cost, and storage requires Penning traps holding tiny quantities.
